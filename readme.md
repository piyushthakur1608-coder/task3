BUSINESS PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS REPORT

1. Executive Summary

This report evaluates the organization's sales and profitability
performance using advanced SQL analysis, Excel variance analysis,
and an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

The analysis focuses on monthly sales growth, regional performance,
category growth, customer segment contribution, and the relationship
between discount levels and profitability.


2. Month-over-Month Performance

Monthly sales were analyzed using current-month sales, previous-month
sales, sales variance, and growth percentage.

Growth % =
(Current Month Sales - Previous Month Sales)
/
Previous Month Sales × 100

Months with positive growth percentages indicate expansion, while
negative values indicate sales decline.

Management should investigate months showing consecutive negative
growth and compare them with category, regional, and customer-segment
performance.


3. Regional Performance

Regional performance was evaluated using total sales, total profit,
and profit margin.

The region with the lowest total profit or weakest profit margin
should be considered underperforming.

Management should investigate:

• regional sales volume
• customer retention
• discount strategy
• product category mix
• operating and delivery costs


4. Category Growth Analysis

Category performance was analyzed using sales, profit, profit margin,
and month-over-month growth.

The category with the highest sustainable positive growth should
receive additional investment in marketing, inventory, and customer
acquisition.

Management should avoid relying only on revenue growth and should
also check profitability.


5. Discount and Profitability Analysis

Orders were grouped into no-discount, low-discount, medium-discount,
and high-discount groups.

The analysis compares average profit and total profit for each group.

If higher discount groups consistently produce lower profit margins
or losses, the company should introduce category-specific discount
limits instead of using broad discount campaigns.


6. Customer Segment Contribution

Customer segments were compared based on total revenue contribution.

The highest-contributing segment should receive targeted retention
campaigns, loyalty benefits, and personalized offers.

Lower-performing segments should be studied to identify opportunities
for conversion and increased purchase frequency.


7. Strategic Recommendations

Based on the performance analysis, management should:

1. Investigate regions with weak profitability.
2. Increase investment in high-growth and profitable categories.
3. Reduce excessive discounting on low-margin products.
4. Focus retention campaigns on high-value customer segments.
5. Track month-over-month growth using automated KPI dashboards.
6. Review negative-profit orders regularly.
7. Use data-driven pricing and promotion strategies.


8. Conclusion

The dashboard and analytical model provide management with a structured
view of sales growth, profitability, regional performance, category
performance, and customer contribution.

Regular monitoring of these KPIs can help management identify declining
performance early and make more effective strategic decisions.
